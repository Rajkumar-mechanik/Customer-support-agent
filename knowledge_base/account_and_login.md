# Account and Login Help

## Resetting a forgotten password
1. Open the Brightwell login page and choose Forgot password.
2. Enter the email address or mobile number registered to your account.
3. We send a reset link by email or a one-time code by SMS. The link or code is valid for 15 minutes.
4. Open the link or enter the code and set a new password. It must have at least 10 characters, including a number and a symbol.
5. Log in with the new password.
If you do not receive the email, check your spam folder and wait 5 minutes before requesting again. You can request a reset up to 3 times per hour.

## Account locked after failed attempts
After 5 incorrect password attempts the account is locked for 30 minutes. You can wait for the lock to expire or reset your password to unlock it immediately.

## One-time code not arriving
Make sure your phone has network coverage and that SMS from commercial senders is not blocked by Do Not Disturb settings. You can request a new code up to 3 times. After that, wait 30 minutes before trying again, or choose the email option instead.

## Changing your email address or mobile number
Open Account, then Profile, and choose Edit next to the email or mobile number. We first send a code to your current contact and then to the new one. You must confirm both. If you no longer have access to your current email or number, contact Brightwell Support and we will verify your identity using details of your most recent order.

## Two-step verification
You can turn on two-step verification under Account, then Security, using an authenticator app or SMS. If you lose the device that holds your authenticator, contact Brightwell Support to disable it after identity verification.

## Keeping your account safe
Brightwell staff will never ask for your password, one-time code, card number or CVV by chat, phone, email or social media. If someone asks, do not share it. If you think someone else has accessed your account, reset your password straight away, choose Sign out of all devices under Account, then Security, and contact Brightwell Support.

## Deleting your account
Open Account, then Privacy, and choose Delete my account. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Any open orders, returns or warranty claims must be finished first. Order invoices are retained for the period required by tax law.

## Guest checkout and order lookup
You can check out as a guest using your email address. To look up a guest order, use Track Order on the home page with your order ID and the email used at checkout.
