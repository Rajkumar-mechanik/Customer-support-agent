# Billing and Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking, Brightwell Wallet and cash on delivery. Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹20,000 in serviceable pin codes. No-cost EMI for 3 and 6 months is available on selected credit cards for orders of ₹5,000 and above.

## Payment failed but money was deducted
If a payment fails at checkout but the amount is deducted from your account, the bank reverses it automatically within 5 to 7 business days. You do not need to contact us unless the amount has not returned after 7 business days. If the order shows as confirmed in My Orders, the payment succeeded and no reversal is due.

## Charged more than once, duplicate or double charges
First open My Orders and check whether the extra charges belong to separate orders. A pending authorisation hold from a failed attempt can look like a second charge, and holds drop off within 5 to 7 business days. If two or more payments were completed for the same order, the Billing Team reviews the case within 2 business days. Confirmed duplicate payments are refunded to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days of the review.

## Disputes with your bank and chargebacks
If you have contacted your bank to dispute a charge, please also write to us with your order ID so the Billing Team can review the payment. We review disputed and duplicate payments within 2 business days. A bank chargeback can delay a refund because the bank holds the amount during its own review. If you have involved a lawyer, email legal@brightwell.example and our legal team will respond. Our support team cannot discuss legal matters.

## Invoices and GST
Download your invoice from My Orders by selecting the order and choosing Download Invoice. To receive a GST invoice with your business GSTIN, add the GSTIN at checkout. A GSTIN cannot be added after the order is placed.

## Coupons and offers
Only one coupon can be applied to an order and coupons cannot be combined with other discount codes. A coupon cannot be added after the payment is completed. Offer prices are not adjusted retrospectively when a price drops after you buy.

## Brightwell Wallet
Refunds sent to the Brightwell Wallet are credited within 24 hours of approval. Wallet balance does not expire for 12 months from the last credit and cannot be withdrawn to a bank account.

## Payment security
Payments are processed on a PCI-DSS compliant payment page. Brightwell never stores your full card number or CVV and never asks for them by chat, phone or email. If you receive a message asking for card details that claims to be from Brightwell, do not respond and report it to Brightwell Support.

## Cash on delivery orders
For cash on delivery you pay the courier in cash or by UPI at the time of delivery. Keep the exact amount ready because couriers may not carry change.
