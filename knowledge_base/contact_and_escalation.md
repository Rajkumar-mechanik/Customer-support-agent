# Contact and Escalation Information

## How to reach Brightwell Support
Live chat is available in the app and on the website from 9 AM to 9 PM IST, Monday to Saturday. Email support@brightwell.example and we reply within 24 hours. Phone support is on 1800 120 4455, from 9 AM to 6 PM IST, Monday to Saturday. Please keep your order ID ready.

## What happens when a ticket is escalated
The AI support desk answers common questions from our documentation. When a case is urgent, involves a payment dispute, a legal or safety matter, or is something our documentation does not cover, it is passed to a human specialist along with the full conversation, so you do not need to repeat yourself. Escalated tickets are reviewed by a human specialist within 1 business day, and high-urgency tickets within 4 business hours.

## Your ticket reference
Every request receives a ticket ID in the format TKT followed by a number. Quote this ID in any follow-up so the team can find your case straight away.

## Legal notices
Formal legal notices, including consumer forum and consumer court complaints, are handled by our legal team and should be emailed to legal@brightwell.example. Our support team cannot respond to legal matters directly, but will route your message to the right team.

## Feedback and complaints about service
If you are unhappy with how a request was handled, reply to the ticket email and ask for a supervisor review. A senior team member responds within 2 business days.

## Business hours and holidays
Brightwell is closed on national public holidays. Orders can still be placed on those days, but dispatch, refunds and support replies resume on the next business day.
