# Order Changes and Cancellations

## Cancelling before dispatch
You can cancel an order for free from My Orders until it is dispatched. Select the order and choose Cancel Order. Refunds for cancelled prepaid orders reach your original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Refunds to a Brightwell Wallet are credited within 24 hours.

## Cancelling after dispatch
Once an order is dispatched it cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the delivery when the courier arrives, and the order is refunded after the parcel returns to our warehouse, or you can accept it and return it within the 10-day return window.

## Changing items, colour or quantity
Items in a confirmed order cannot be edited. To change an item, cancel the order before dispatch and place a new order. Discounts and coupons applied to the cancelled order are not transferable.

## Order confirmation
You receive an order confirmation email and SMS within 15 minutes of a successful payment. If you do not see it, check your spam folder and then look in My Orders. If the order is not listed there, the payment did not complete.

## Items that go out of stock
If an item becomes unavailable after you order it, we cancel the order automatically, send you an email, and refund the full amount within 5 to 7 business days.

## Wrong item received
If you receive a different product from the one you ordered, report it within 10 days of delivery with photos of the item and the label. We arrange a free pickup and ship the correct item within 3 business days, or refund you in full.

## Missing items in the parcel
If an accessory or one of the items in a multi-item order is missing, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the opened parcel and the packing slip. Missing accessories are sent free of charge after verification.
