# Product Care and Safety Guide

## Battery safety warning signs
If a product with a lithium battery becomes very hot, swells, smells of burning, leaks or emits smoke, stop using it at once. Unplug it from the charger, move it away from flammable items and do not try to charge or open it. Contact Brightwell Support immediately. We treat these reports as high priority, arrange a free priority pickup and replace the product after inspection.

## Using the right charger
Use only the charger and cable supplied in the box or a certified accessory of the same rating. Damage caused by uncertified chargers is not covered by the warranty. Do not charge devices under a pillow or on a bed, and do not leave them charging in direct sunlight.

## Brightwell Home app
Smart devices are set up with the Brightwell Home app, available for Android 9 and above and iOS 14 and above. Smart devices connect only to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi networks. If your router combines 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz under one name, temporarily turn off 5 GHz during setup.

## Firmware updates
Open the Brightwell Home app, choose the device, then Settings and Firmware update. Keep the device powered and connected during an update, which takes up to 10 minutes. Interrupting an update can leave the device unresponsive, and holding the reset button for 10 seconds restores it.

## Water and dust resistance
IPX4 means splash resistant, not waterproof. IPX7 means the product survives immersion in up to 1 metre of fresh water for 30 minutes. Resistance reduces over time and liquid damage is not covered by the warranty beyond the stated rating.

## Operating and storage conditions
Use and store Brightwell products between 0 °C and 35 °C. Avoid leaving devices in a parked car in summer. Store batteries at about 50 percent charge if the product will not be used for more than 30 days.

## Cleaning
Unplug the product before cleaning and use a soft, slightly damp cloth. Do not use alcohol, solvents or abrasive pads on screens or finishes. Do not spray liquid directly onto any product.

## Recycling and e-waste
When you buy a replacement you can hand over the old electronic product to our courier at no cost for responsible recycling. Please do not throw batteries or electronics in household waste.
