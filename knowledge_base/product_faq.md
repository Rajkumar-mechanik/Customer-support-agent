# Product FAQ

## Where do I find the serial number
The serial number is printed on a label on the product, usually on the back or base, and on the box next to the barcode. For earbuds it is inside the charging case lid. For the Pulse smartwatch it is under Settings, then About, on the watch. You need the serial number for a warranty claim.

## Which products work with the Brightwell Home app
The Brightwell Home app works with the Aero Wireless Earbuds, Pulse Smartwatch, Glide Action Camera, Lumen Smart LED Bulbs, Hub Mini Smart Speaker, Guard Security Camera, Sweep R1 Robot Vacuum, Brew Smart Coffee Maker, Pure Air Purifier and Stream Wi-Fi 6 Router. Other products do not need the app. The app runs on Android 9 and above and iOS 14 and above.

## Which products need 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi
The Lumen bulbs, Guard camera, Sweep R1 robot vacuum, Brew coffee maker and Pure air purifier connect only to 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi during setup. The Hub Mini speaker works on both 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz. If your router uses one name for both bands, switch off 5 GHz temporarily while you add the device.

## Water resistance of our products
The Aero earbuds are IPX4, which means splash resistant. The Pulse smartwatch is rated 5 ATM and is suitable for swimming in a pool. The Glide action camera is waterproof to 10 metres without a case. The other products have no water resistance rating and must be kept dry.

## Warranty periods at a glance
Most electronics carry 12 months. The Volt power bank carries 18 months. The Whisk Pro blender motor, Crisp air fryer, Sweep R1 robot vacuum, Breeze tower fan and Pure air purifier carry 24 months. The View monitor and Stream router carry 36 months. The Stride shoes carry 6 months. Batteries, filters, jars, blades and other accessories carry 6 months. The full rules are in the Replacement and Warranty Policy.

## Do I need the original box
Keep the box for the first 10 days, because a change-of-mind return needs the original packaging. A warranty claim after that does not need the box, but it does need the serial number and proof of purchase.

## Are accessories and spare parts available
Replacement filters for the Pure air purifier, brushes and mop pads for the Sweep R1 robot vacuum, ear tips for the Aero earbuds, straps for the Pulse smartwatch and spare switches for the Type keyboard are sold in the Brightwell store. They are consumables and are not covered by the product warranty.

## Can I use a third-party charger
Use the charger in the box or a certified charger of the same rating. Third-party chargers that do not meet the rating can damage the battery, and damage caused by an uncertified charger is not covered by the warranty.

## Do products work outside India
Brightwell products are designed for Indian mains voltage of 230 V and Indian plug types. We do not ship outside India and warranty service is available only in India.
