# Brightwell Aero Wireless Earbuds (BW-EB200)

## Overview
True wireless earbuds with active noise cancellation, a pocket-sized charging case and a companion app for equaliser presets. The Brightwell price is ₹3,499. Warranty: 12 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Bluetooth 5.3 with a range of about 10 metres
- Battery: 7 hours per charge with noise cancellation off, 5 hours with it on
- Charging case adds 3 full charges for 28 hours in total
- Charging time: 1.5 hours for the case, 1 hour for the earbuds via USB-C
- Water resistance: IPX4 (splash resistant, not waterproof)
- Weight: 5 grams per earbud

## What is in the box
- Two earbuds
- Charging case
- USB-C cable
- 3 sizes of silicone ear tips (S, M, L)
- Quick start guide

## Setup
1. Open the case lid with the earbuds inside; the LED blinks white.
2. Open Bluetooth settings on your phone and select Brightwell Aero.
3. For the app, install Brightwell Home and follow Add Device to enable equaliser presets and firmware updates.

## Troubleshooting
### Only one earbud plays sound
Put both earbuds in the case, close the lid for 10 seconds, then open it and hold both touch panels for 5 seconds until the LED flashes red and white. Delete Brightwell Aero from your phone's Bluetooth list and pair again.

### Earbuds will not charge in the case
Wipe the metal contacts on the earbuds and in the case with a dry cloth. The case LED should glow amber while charging. If the case LED stays off, charge the case for 30 minutes with a different USB-C cable and adapter.

### Earbuds keep disconnecting
Keep the phone and earbuds within 10 metres without a wall between them, turn off other Bluetooth devices nearby, and update the firmware in the Brightwell Home app.

### Noise cancellation sounds weak
Try a different ear tip size so the earbud seals in your ear. Hold the left panel for 2 seconds to cycle between noise cancellation, transparency and off.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can I use just one earbud?
Yes. Either earbud works alone in mono mode. Place the other one in the case.

### Are the earbuds waterproof?
No. They are IPX4 rated, which covers sweat and light rain. Do not swim or shower with them.

### How do I factory reset the earbuds?
Put both earbuds in the case, keep the lid open and hold the button on the back of the case for 15 seconds until the LED flashes red three times.

## Returns, replacement and warranty notes
Because they are in-ear products, Aero earbuds can be returned for a change of mind only if the hygiene seal is intact. Defective units are always eligible for replacement. The standard 10-day return window and the replacement policy apply to this product, and the warranty is 12 months.
