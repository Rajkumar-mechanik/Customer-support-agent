# Brightwell Breeze Tower Fan (BW-TF30)

## Overview
A slim 30 inch oscillating tower fan with an energy-saving BLDC motor, remote control and a 12 hour timer. The Brightwell price is ₹4,799. Warranty: 24 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Height: 30 inches (76 cm)
- Motor: BLDC with 12 speed levels, power use from 4 W to 38 W
- Oscillation: 80 degrees
- Modes: normal, natural wind and sleep
- Timer: 1 to 12 hours
- Noise: 28 dB at the lowest speed

## What is in the box
- Fan tower
- Base and screws
- Remote control with battery
- User manual

## Setup
1. Attach the base to the tower using the 4 screws provided and check it stands steady.
2. Insert the remote battery and point the remote at the display.
3. Plug in and press the power button to start on speed 1.

## Troubleshooting
### Fan makes a rattling noise
Check that the base screws are fully tightened and the fan stands on a level surface. Wipe dust from the grille, since build-up can create noise.

### Remote does not work
Replace the remote battery and clear any obstruction between the remote and the display. The fan still works with the buttons on top.

### Fan does not oscillate
Press the oscillation button on the remote or panel. Make sure nothing blocks the base and that the fan is not on a soft carpet.

### Fan switches off by itself
Check whether a timer is set, and press the timer button until it shows zero. Sleep mode also lowers speed and may turn the fan off after a while.

## Frequently asked questions
### How do I clean the fan?
Unplug it and wipe the grille with a soft dry cloth. Never spray water inside.

### Does it use a lot of electricity?
At speed 1 it uses about 4 W, and at the highest speed 38 W.

### Can it be wall-mounted?
No. It is designed only for floor use.

## Returns, replacement and warranty notes
Keep the fan away from water. Damage caused by water entering the motor housing is not covered. The standard 10-day return window and the replacement policy apply to this product, and the warranty is 24 months.
