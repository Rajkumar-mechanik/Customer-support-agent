# Brightwell Brew Smart Coffee Maker (BW-CM12)

## Overview
A 12-cup drip coffee maker with a built-in grinder, programmable brew schedule and app control. The Brightwell price is ₹6,499. Warranty: 12 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Capacity: 1.5 litre water tank, 12 cups
- Built-in conical burr grinder with 5 grind settings
- Heating: 1000 W with a thermal carafe that keeps coffee hot for 4 hours
- Connectivity: 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi for scheduling in the Brightwell Home app
- Auto shut-off: 30 minutes after brewing
- Dimensions: 38 cm tall by 20 cm wide

## What is in the box
- Coffee maker

## Setup
1. Rinse the carafe and filter basket and run one brew cycle with water only.
2. Add whole beans to the hopper and set the grind size, using a coarser setting for strong flavour.
3. Open the Brightwell Home app to set a daily brew schedule.

## Troubleshooting
### Coffee tastes weak
Use more beans per cup, a finer grind and fresh beans. Descale the machine if it has been used for more than a month.

### Machine brews slowly or the water stops
Limescale is blocking the heater. Run the descaling programme: mix the descaling sachet with 500 ml of water, start Descale in the menu and rinse with two full tanks of fresh water afterwards.

### Grinder jams
Switch off and unplug the machine. Empty the hopper and remove the burr cover to clear trapped beans. Do not use oily or flavoured beans, which clog the grinder.

### Scheduled brews are not starting
Make sure the machine is plugged in, has water, and shows online in the Brightwell Home app. Check that the app time zone is set correctly.

## Frequently asked questions
### How often should I descale it?
Every 4 weeks, or every 2 weeks if you have hard water.

### Can I use pre-ground coffee?
Yes. Switch off the grinder from the menu and add ground coffee to the filter basket.

### Is the carafe dishwasher safe?
The lid and filter basket are top-rack dishwasher safe. Hand-wash the carafe body.
