# Brightwell Core Wireless Mouse (BW-MS55)

## Overview
A silent-click wireless mouse with a 2.4 GHz USB receiver, Bluetooth 5.0 and an adjustable 800 to 3200 DPI sensor. The Brightwell price is ₹999. Warranty: 12 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Connectivity: 2.4 GHz USB receiver and Bluetooth 5.0, switchable between 2 devices
- Sensor: optical, 800, 1200, 1600 and 3200 DPI
- Battery: 1 AA battery lasting up to 12 months
- Wireless range: 10 metres
- Buttons: 5 including silent left and right click
- Weight: 85 grams with battery

## What is in the box
- Mouse
- USB receiver stored in the battery bay
- AA battery
- Quick start guide

## Setup
1. Insert the AA battery and remove the USB receiver from the battery bay.
2. For 2.4 GHz plug the receiver into a USB port and switch the mouse on.
3. For Bluetooth press the connect button under the mouse for 3 seconds and select Core Mouse.

## Troubleshooting
### Cursor lags or jumps
Plug the receiver into a port close to the mouse, away from USB 3.0 hubs and Wi-Fi routers. Replace the battery. Use a mouse pad because glossy glass surfaces can affect the sensor.

### Mouse is not detected
Make sure the battery is the right way round, switch the mouse off and on, and try another USB port. For Bluetooth, delete the mouse from the device list and pair again.

### Scroll wheel is not working
Clean around the wheel with a dry brush or compressed air. If it still fails within the warranty period, contact Brightwell Support.

### Double-click happens on a single click
Update your operating system mouse driver and lower the double-click speed in settings. If it continues, it is a switch fault covered by the warranty.

## Frequently asked questions
### Which batteries should I use?
Use one AA alkaline battery. Rechargeable batteries work but may last for a shorter time.

### Can I connect it to two computers?
Yes. Use the connect button to switch between the receiver and Bluetooth.

### Does it work with a Mac?
Yes, using Bluetooth. The USB receiver also works through a USB-C adapter.
