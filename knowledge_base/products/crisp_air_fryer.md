## Setup
1. Wash the basket in warm soapy water and dry thoroughly.
2. Run the empty air fryer at 200 °C for 5 minutes to burn off the protective coating, which may smell briefly.
3. Preheat for 3 minutes and then add food.

## Troubleshooting
### White smoke comes out while cooking
Fatty food such as bacon releases oil that smokes at high temperature. Add a little water in the drawer under the basket, lower the temperature and clean the heating element.

### Food is not crispy
Do not overfill the basket. Shake food halfway through cooking, pat food dry and brush with a teaspoon of oil. Increase the temperature by 10 °C if needed.

### Air fryer does not turn on
Check that the basket is pushed fully in because the safety switch stops operation when it is open. Try another power socket.

### Error code E1 or E2 appears
These codes mean a temperature sensor fault. Unplug the unit for 10 minutes and restart. If the code returns, contact Brightwell Support with the serial number for a warranty claim.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can I use aluminium foil?
Yes in small pieces, never covering the whole basket, and never during preheating.

### Is the basket dishwasher safe?
Yes, but handwashing extends the life of the non-stick coating.

### Does it need preheating?
A 3 minute preheat gives crispier results for most foods.
