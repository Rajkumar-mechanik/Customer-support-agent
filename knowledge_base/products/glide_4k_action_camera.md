# Brightwell Glide 4K Action Camera (BW-AC400)

## Overview
A 4K 60 fps action camera with electronic image stabilisation, a waterproof body and a dual-screen design. The Brightwell price is ₹8,499. Warranty: 12 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Video: 4K at 60 fps, 2.7K at 120 fps and 1080p at 240 fps
- Photo: 20 megapixels
- Waterproof to 10 metres without a case
- Battery: 1750 mAh, about 100 minutes of 4K 30 fps recording
- Storage: microSD card up to 256 GB, UHS-I U3 or better
- Screens: 2 inch rear touchscreen and 1.4 inch front screen

## What is in the box
- Glide camera
- 1750 mAh battery
- USB-C cable
- Mounting frame and 2 mounts
- Lens cloth
- Quick start guide

## Setup
1. Insert the battery and a microSD card, then format the card in the camera under Settings.
2. Pair with the Brightwell Home app over Wi-Fi for preview and transfer.
3. Use a UHS-I U3 card or better for 4K recording.

## Troubleshooting
### Camera shows Card Error
Format the microSD card in the camera menu, which erases it, so back up files first. Use a branded UHS-I U3 card. Cheap or counterfeit cards often fail at 4K.

### Camera stops recording by itself
Recording stops when the battery is low, the card is full or the camera overheats. In warm weather use 2.7K or turn off stabilisation and let it cool for 10 minutes.

### Wi-Fi connection to the app drops
Keep the phone within 5 metres, turn off mobile data saver and reconnect to the camera's own Wi-Fi network from the app.

### Footage looks blurry underwater
Wipe the lens and wait for the camera to reach water temperature to avoid fogging. Use video modes at 1080p or 4K for best clarity.

## Frequently asked questions
### How deep can the Glide go without a case?
10 metres. Close the battery door lock firmly and check the seal is clean before every dive.

### Can it record while charging?
Yes, with a power source connected, for continuous recording such as a dashcam use.

### Does it support a tripod?
Yes, use the included mounting frame which has a standard 1/4 inch thread.

## Returns, replacement and warranty notes
Rinse with fresh water after use in salt water or chlorine and dry the seals. Water damage caused by a dirty or damaged seal is not covered. The standard 10-day return window and the replacement policy apply to this product, and the warranty is 12 months.
