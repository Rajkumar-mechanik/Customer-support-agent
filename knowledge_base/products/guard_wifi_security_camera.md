# Brightwell Guard Wi-Fi Security Camera (BW-SC20)

## Overview
An indoor 2K pan and tilt Wi-Fi camera with night vision, two-way audio and motion alerts. The Brightwell price is ₹2,999. Warranty: 12 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Resolution: 2K (3 megapixel) at 20 fps
- Field of view: 110 degrees, with 355 degree pan and 90 degree tilt
- Night vision: infrared up to 10 metres
- Storage: microSD up to 256 GB or optional cloud plan
- Connectivity: 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only
- Power: 5 V USB adapter with a 2 metre cable, indoor use only

## What is in the box
- Camera
- 5 V USB adapter and cable
- Mounting plate and screws
- Quick start guide

## Setup
1. Plug in the camera and wait for the voice prompt that it is ready to pair.
2. Open the Brightwell Home app, choose Add Device, then Camera, and scan the QR code shown by the app with the camera lens.
3. Insert a microSD card to record locally.

## Troubleshooting
### Camera is offline
Check the camera has power and the router is working. The camera supports only 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Move it closer to the router and restart both.

### Night vision image is blurry or white
Remove window glass in front of the lens, because infrared reflects off glass and washes out the picture. Keep the camera at least 30 cm from walls.

### Motion alerts are too frequent
Reduce motion sensitivity and set up activity zones in the app so that fans, curtains and pets are ignored.

### Camera will not record to the card
Format the microSD card in the app under Storage and use a card of 256 GB or less in class 10 or better.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is the video encrypted?
Yes. Video is encrypted in transit between the camera and the app.

### Can I use it outdoors?
No. It is designed for indoor use only and has no weather resistance.

### Can two family members view it?
Yes. Share the device from the Brightwell Home app with up to 5 people.

## Returns, replacement and warranty notes
Cameras installed outdoors or in wet conditions are not covered by the warranty. The standard 10-day return window and the replacement policy apply to this product, and the warranty is 12 months.
