# Brightwell Hub Mini Smart Speaker (BW-SP100)

## Overview
A compact smart speaker with a built-in voice assistant, 360 degree sound and a physical microphone switch. The Brightwell price is ₹3,999. Warranty: 12 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Drivers: 1 full-range 52 mm driver and 2 passive radiators
- Output: 10 W
- Connectivity: 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz Wi-Fi, Bluetooth 5.0
- Microphone: 4-microphone array with a hardware mute switch
- Power: 15 W USB-C adapter, no battery
- Dimensions: 100 mm tall by 90 mm wide

## What is in the box
- Hub Mini speaker
- 15 W USB-C adapter with 1.5 metre cable
- Quick start guide

## Setup
1. Plug in the speaker and wait for the light ring to pulse orange.
2. Open the Brightwell Home app, choose Add Device, then Speaker.
3. Select your Wi-Fi network and sign in to a voice assistant account when asked.

## Troubleshooting
### Speaker does not respond to the wake word
Check that the microphone switch on the back is not set to mute, which shows a red light ring. Move the speaker away from walls and noisy appliances, and say the wake word clearly from within 5 metres.

### Speaker keeps saying it is not connected
Restart the router and the speaker. If the speaker sits far from the router, move it closer. Make sure the Wi-Fi network does not require a web login page.

### Sound is distorted at high volume
Place the speaker on a flat surface away from corners and reduce bass in the equaliser. Keep the volume below 90 percent.

### Bluetooth pairing fails
Say Pair Bluetooth or hold the Bluetooth button for 3 seconds, and remove the speaker from your phone's saved devices before trying again.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can the speaker work as a stereo pair?
Yes. Two Hub Mini speakers can be grouped as left and right in the Brightwell Home app.

### Is it always listening?
It listens only for the wake word on the device. The hardware mute switch physically disconnects the microphones.

### Does it run on a battery?
No. It must stay plugged in.

## Returns, replacement and warranty notes
Keep the speaker away from water and heat. It has no water resistance rating. The standard 10-day return window and the replacement policy apply to this product, and the warranty is 12 months.
