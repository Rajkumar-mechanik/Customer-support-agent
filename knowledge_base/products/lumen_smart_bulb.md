# Brightwell Lumen Smart LED Bulb 4-pack (BW-LB04)

## Setup
1. Screw in the bulb and switch on. It blinks quickly when ready to pair.
2. Open the Brightwell Home app, choose Add Device and select Lighting.
3. Enter your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi password and wait for the bulb to turn solid white.

## Troubleshooting
### Bulb will not connect to Wi-Fi
Smart bulbs support only 2.4 GHz. Move closer to the router, confirm the Wi-Fi password and make sure the bulb is blinking fast. If it is slow or steady, turn the bulb off and on three times to reset it.

### Bulb shows offline in the app
Check that the wall switch is on and the router is working. Smart bulbs must stay powered, so do not turn them off at the switch. Restart the router and wait 2 minutes.

### Bulb flickers or buzzes
Flicker usually means the bulb is on a dimmer switch. Lumen bulbs are not compatible with wall dimmers. Dim them in the app instead.

### Colours look different between bulbs
Update every bulb to the latest firmware in the app and group them so they change together.

## Frequently asked questions
### Do I need a hub?
No. The bulbs connect directly to your 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi.

### Does the bulb remember its state after a power cut?
Yes. In the app you can choose whether it returns to on, off or the last state.

### Can I control the bulbs when I am away from home?
Yes. With internet access you can control them from anywhere in the Brightwell Home app.
