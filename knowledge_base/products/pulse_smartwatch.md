# Brightwell Pulse Smartwatch (BW-SW310)

## Overview
A 1.4 inch AMOLED smartwatch with heart-rate and sleep tracking, built-in GPS and 40 sports modes. The Brightwell price is ₹5,999. Warranty: 12 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Display: 1.4 inch AMOLED, 454 by 454 pixels
- Battery: up to 10 days typical use, 14 hours with continuous GPS
- Charging time: 2 hours with the magnetic charging cradle
- Water resistance: 5 ATM, suitable for swimming in a pool
- Sensors: optical heart rate, SpO2, accelerometer, gyroscope and GPS
- Works with Android 9 and above and iOS 14 and above

## What is in the box
- Pulse smartwatch with silicone strap
- Magnetic charging cradle
- Quick start guide

## Setup
1. Charge the watch for 30 minutes before first use.
2. Install the Brightwell Home app and choose Add Device, then Smartwatch.
3. Keep the watch next to the phone and confirm the pairing code shown on the watch screen.

## Troubleshooting
### Watch does not charge
Clean the gold contacts on the back of the watch and the cradle, align the magnets and use a 5 V adapter. The battery icon appears within 10 minutes. Try a different USB port or adapter if it does not.

### Notifications are not arriving
In the Brightwell Home app allow notification access and set battery optimisation to Unrestricted for the app. On iPhone, enable Show Notifications for each app under Bluetooth settings.

### Heart-rate reading looks wrong
Wear the watch one finger-width above the wrist bone, snug but not tight, and keep the sensor clean and dry. Readings are less accurate during very vigorous wrist movement.

### Screen is blank
Press and hold the crown for 10 seconds to force a restart. If that does not help, place the watch on the charger for 15 minutes and try again.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can I swim with the Pulse?
Yes, in a pool and in the shower. It is rated 5 ATM. Avoid hot water, saunas and high-speed water sports.

### Does it need a phone to track GPS runs?
No. The built-in GPS records the route on the watch. Sync it with the app afterwards.

### Can I change the strap?
Yes, it uses standard 22 mm quick-release straps.

## Returns, replacement and warranty notes
