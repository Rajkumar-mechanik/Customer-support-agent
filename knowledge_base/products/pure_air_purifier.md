# Brightwell Pure Air Purifier (BW-AP200)

## Overview
A HEPA H13 air purifier for rooms up to 400 sq ft with a PM2.5 sensor, app control and a quiet sleep mode. The Brightwell price is ₹9,999. Warranty: 24 months on the unit and 6 months on the filter from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Coverage: up to 400 sq ft (37 sq m)
- CADR: 360 cubic metres per hour
- Filter: 3-stage with pre-filter, HEPA H13 and activated carbon
- Sensor: laser PM2.5 with colour ring (blue good, orange moderate, red poor)
- Noise: 24 dB in sleep mode
- Filter life: about 6 months or 4320 hours

## What is in the box
- Air purifier
- Pre-installed HEPA H13 filter
- Power cable
- User manual

## Setup
1. Remove all plastic wrapping from the filter before use, because an unopened filter blocks airflow.
2. Place the purifier at least 30 cm from walls and furniture.
3. Add it in the Brightwell Home app over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi to see live air quality.

## Troubleshooting
### Filter light stays red after replacing the filter
Hold the filter reset button for 5 seconds after fitting a new filter until the light turns off.

### Purifier is noisy
Make sure the filter is plastic-free and seated correctly, and the pre-filter is not clogged with dust. Use sleep mode at night.

### Air quality ring stays red
Close windows and doors, and wait 30 minutes. Check that the sensor window on the side is clean by wiping it with a dry cotton swab.

### Bad smell from the purifier
The carbon filter may be saturated. Replace the filter if it has been used for 6 months or more.

## Frequently asked questions
### How often should I replace the filter?
Every 6 months in normal use, or sooner in very polluted areas. The app shows the remaining filter life.

### Can I wash the HEPA filter?
No. Washing damages it. Vacuum the pre-filter every 2 weeks.

### Does it make ozone?
No. It uses mechanical filtration only and produces no ozone.

## Returns, replacement and warranty notes
Filters are consumables with their own 6-month coverage for manufacturing defects only. The standard 10-day return window and the replacement policy apply to this product, and the warranty is 24 months on the unit and 6 months on the filter.
