# Brightwell Slate 10 Tablet (BW-TB10)

## Overview
A 10.4 inch tablet with a 2K display, an octa-core processor, 8 GB RAM and 128 GB storage, ideal for study and streaming. The Brightwell price is ₹16,999. Warranty: 12 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Display: 10.4 inch 2000 by 1200 pixels, 60 Hz
- Processor: octa-core 2.2 GHz, 8 GB RAM, 128 GB storage expandable to 1 TB with microSD
- Battery: 7040 mAh, up to 12 hours of video playback
- Charging: 18 W USB-C, about 3 hours for a full charge
- Cameras: 8 MP rear and 5 MP front
- Connectivity: Wi-Fi 5 and Bluetooth 5.0, no SIM slot

## What is in the box
- Tablet
- 18 W USB-C adapter and cable
- SIM-free quick start guide

## Setup
1. Charge for 30 minutes and hold the power button for 3 seconds to start.
2. Connect to Wi-Fi and sign in to a Google account.
3. Install updates under Settings, then System, then System update.

## Troubleshooting
### Tablet will not turn on
Charge it for at least 30 minutes with the original adapter. Then hold power and volume down together for 15 seconds to force a restart.

### Tablet charges very slowly
Use the 18 W adapter and cable from the box. Use of low-power adapters can take over 6 hours. Heavy gaming while charging also slows charging.

### Wi-Fi keeps disconnecting
Forget the network and reconnect. Restart the router. If the problem continues, update the tablet and reset network settings under System.

### Touchscreen is unresponsive
Remove the screen protector if it is thick or damaged and clean the screen. Restart the tablet. If a small area still fails, contact Brightwell Support for a warranty check.

## Frequently asked questions
### Does the tablet support a SIM?
No. It is a Wi-Fi only tablet.

### Can I expand the storage?
Yes, with a microSD card up to 1 TB.

### Does it support a stylus?
Any passive capacitive stylus works. There is no pressure-sensitive pen support.

## Returns, replacement and warranty notes
Cracked screens caused by drops are not covered. Back up your data before sending a tablet for repair because a factory reset may be required. The standard 10-day return window and the replacement policy apply to this product, and the warranty is 12 months.
