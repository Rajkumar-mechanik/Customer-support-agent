# Brightwell Stream Wi-Fi 6 Router (BW-RT600)

## Overview
An AX1800 dual-band Wi-Fi 6 router with 4 gigabit LAN ports, parental controls and mesh expansion support. The Brightwell price is ₹5,999. Warranty: 36 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Speed: AX1800, up to 574 Mbps on 2.4 GHz and 1201 Mbps on 5 GHz
- Ports: 1 gigabit WAN and 4 gigabit LAN
- Coverage: up to 1500 sq ft
- Security: WPA3 and a guest network
- Antennas: 4 external high-gain antennas
- Management: Brightwell Home app with parental controls

## What is in the box
- Router
- 12 V power adapter
- Ethernet cable
- Quick start guide

## Setup
1. Connect the modem to the blue WAN port with the Ethernet cable and switch on the router.
2. Wait 2 minutes for the light to turn solid white, then join the Wi-Fi name printed on the label.
3. Open the Brightwell Home app to set your own network name and password.

## Troubleshooting
### No internet but Wi-Fi is connected
Restart the modem for 30 seconds, then restart the router. Check that the WAN cable is in the blue port and that your internet service is active.

### Slow Wi-Fi speeds
Connect to the 5 GHz network near the router and place the router in the open away from walls and microwaves. Change the Wi-Fi channel in the app.

### Router light is red
A red light means the router is not receiving internet. Check the WAN cable and contact your internet provider if the modem has no signal.

### I forgot the router password
Press and hold the reset button for 10 seconds with a pin. This restores factory settings and the default password printed on the label.

## Frequently asked questions
### Does the router work with all internet providers?
Yes. It supports dynamic IP, static IP and PPPoE connections.

### Can I add a second router for mesh?
Yes. Two Stream routers can form a mesh network in the Brightwell Home app.

### Does it support IPv6?
Yes, IPv6 can be turned on in the app under Internet settings.
