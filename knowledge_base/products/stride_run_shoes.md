# Brightwell Stride Run Shoes (BW-FS410)

## Overview
Lightweight everyday running shoes with a breathable mesh upper, a cushioned foam midsole and a durable rubber outsole. The Brightwell price is ₹3,299. Warranty: 6 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Upper: engineered breathable mesh
- Midsole: cushioned EVA foam, 8 mm heel-to-toe drop
- Outsole: carbon rubber with a grip pattern
- Weight: about 260 grams for a UK size 8
- Sizes: UK 6 to 12 in full sizes, available in black, grey and blue
- Use: road running and gym training

## What is in the box
- One pair of shoes
- Spare laces
- Shoe box and care card

## Setup
1. Try the shoes on indoors on a clean surface, with the socks you would run in.
2. Allow a thumb's width of space in front of the longest toe.
3. Wear them for short runs first to break them in.

## Troubleshooting
### Shoes feel too tight or too loose
You can exchange a size within 10 days of delivery if the shoes are unworn and have the tags attached, subject to stock.

### Sole is coming off
Separation of the sole within 6 months of delivery is a manufacturing defect. Contact Brightwell Support with photos for a warranty claim.

### Shoes smell after use
Air them after every run, remove the insoles to dry and wash with mild soap. Do not put them in a washing machine or dryer.

### Colour is fading
Mesh colours can fade with strong sunlight and frequent washing. Fading is normal wear and is not covered by the warranty.

## Frequently asked questions
### How do I return the shoes?
Within 10 days of delivery, unworn with tags and the box. Raise the return in My Orders and choose a free pickup.

### Are they true to size?
They fit true to size. If you are between sizes, choose the larger one.

Yes. Exchanges are accepted within 10 days subject to stock. If the size is unavailable, we refund the order.
