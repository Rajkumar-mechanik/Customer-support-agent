# Brightwell Sweep R1 Robot Vacuum (BW-RV100)

## Overview
A robot vacuum with laser navigation, 4000 Pa suction, mopping and room mapping through the Brightwell Home app. The Brightwell price is ₹14,999. Warranty: 24 months on the robot and 6 months on the battery from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Suction: 4000 Pa
- Battery: 5200 mAh, up to 150 minutes of cleaning on standard mode
- Navigation: LiDAR with room mapping and no-go zones
- Dustbin: 450 ml, water tank 300 ml
- Climbs thresholds up to 2 cm
- Noise: 62 dB on standard mode

## What is in the box
- Robot vacuum
- Charging dock
- Side brushes (2)
- Mop pad and water tank
- HEPA filter
- User manual

## Setup
1. Place the dock against a wall with 0.5 metre free on both sides and 1.5 metres in front.
2. Remove the protective strip from the bumper and charge the robot for 4 hours before the first run.
3. Add the robot in the Brightwell Home app using the 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi password, then run the first cleaning so it can map the home.

## Troubleshooting
### Robot does not return to the dock
Clear 1.5 metres in front of the dock, keep the dock out of direct sunlight and clean the charging contacts. Re-run a mapping clean if furniture moved.

### Robot gets stuck on carpet or cables
Pick up cables, tassels and loose rugs before cleaning and use the no-go zones in the app. Raise the suction setting only on carpets. Clean the wheels if hair has wrapped around them.

### Robot will not connect to Wi-Fi
The robot supports only 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. Hold the Wi-Fi button for 5 seconds until it beeps, then add it again in the app.

### Error 1 or lidar blocked
Wipe the laser turret on top of the robot and make sure nothing is pressing on it. Restart the robot by holding the power button for 10 seconds.

## Frequently asked questions
### How often do I empty the dustbin?
After every one or two cleans. Clean the HEPA filter monthly and replace it every 6 months.

### Can it clean multiple floors?
Yes. The app stores up to 3 floor maps.

### Can I stop it from entering a room?
Yes. Draw a no-go zone or a virtual wall in the app map.

## Returns, replacement and warranty notes
Brushes, filters and mop pads are consumables and are not covered by the warranty. Replacements are sold separately. The standard 10-day return window and the replacement policy apply to this product, and the warranty is 24 months on the robot and 6 months on the battery.
