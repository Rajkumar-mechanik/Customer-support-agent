# Brightwell Trek 40L Travel Backpack (BW-BP40)

## Overview
A 40 litre carry-on travel backpack with a water-resistant fabric, a padded 16 inch laptop sleeve and a clamshell opening. The Brightwell price is ₹3,799. Warranty: 12 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Capacity: 40 litres, within common cabin baggage size limits
- Fabric: 600D water-resistant polyester with YKK zips
- Laptop sleeve: padded, fits up to 16 inch laptops
- Dimensions: 55 cm by 35 cm by 20 cm
- Weight: 1.2 kg empty
- Extras: hidden passport pocket, luggage strap and USB pass-through port

## What is in the box
- Backpack
- Rain cover
- Care card

## Setup
1. Adjust the shoulder straps and chest strap so the load rides high on your back.
2. Use the compression straps to keep the bag compact for cabin size limits.
3. Attach the rain cover from the bottom pocket in heavy rain.

## Troubleshooting
### Zip is stuck or splitting
Do not force it. Move the slider back, free the fabric from the teeth and rub a little candle wax or zip lubricant on the teeth. Zips that split within the warranty period are covered.

### Water leaks inside the bag
The fabric is water resistant, not waterproof, so use the rain cover in heavy rain and keep electronics in the padded sleeve.

### Strap stitching is coming apart
Stitching failure within 12 months is a manufacturing defect. Send photos to Brightwell Support to start a warranty claim.

### Bag is too big for the airline
The bag complies with most cabin limits when the compression straps are tight. Check your airline's dimensions because limits vary.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can I exchange the colour?
Yes, within 10 days of delivery if the bag is unused and subject to stock.

### Can I machine wash the backpack?
No. Spot clean with mild soap and a damp cloth and air dry.

### Does it have a laptop sleeve?
Yes. It fits laptops up to 16 inches.

Normal wear such as fabric fading or fraying through heavy use is not covered. The backpack carries a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects. The standard 10-day return window and the replacement policy apply to this product, and the warranty is 12 months.
