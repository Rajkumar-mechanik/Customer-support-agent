# Brightwell Type Mechanical Keyboard (BW-KB87)

## Overview
An 87-key tenkeyless mechanical keyboard with hot-swappable switches, per-key RGB lighting and wired or Bluetooth use. The Brightwell price is ₹4,499. Warranty: 12 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Layout: 87 keys, tenkeyless, with a detachable USB-C cable
- Switches: hot-swappable 3-pin or 5-pin, linear red switches installed
- Connectivity: USB-C wired or Bluetooth 5.0 for up to 3 devices
- Battery: 4000 mAh, up to 60 hours without backlight and 12 hours with backlight on
- Lighting: per-key RGB with 12 effects
- Compatibility: Windows, macOS, Android and iOS

## What is in the box
- Keyboard
- USB-C to USB-A cable
- Keycap and switch puller
- Spare switches (2)
- User manual

## Setup
1. Connect with the USB-C cable for wired use.
2. For Bluetooth switch the mode slider to Bluetooth and hold Fn and 1 for 3 seconds to pair device 1.
3. Press Fn and the Windows or Mac key to switch layouts.

## Troubleshooting
### A key does not register
Switch off the keyboard and use the puller to remove the switch, check the pins are straight and press it back firmly. Use a spare switch from the box to confirm.

### Bluetooth keeps disconnecting
Charge the keyboard, delete it from the Bluetooth list and pair again. Keep the keyboard within 5 metres of the computer.

### RGB lighting is off
Press Fn and the right arrow key to increase brightness and Fn and the up arrow key to change the effect. The light turns off after 5 minutes to save battery in Bluetooth mode.

### Keys type the wrong characters on Mac
Hold Fn and the Mac key for 3 seconds to switch to Mac layout, and check the input language in System Settings.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can I use my own switches?
Yes. The board is hot-swappable for most 3-pin and 5-pin MX style switches.

### Is the keyboard backlight adjustable?
Yes. There are 5 brightness levels and 12 effects.

### Can it connect to three devices?
Yes. Use Fn with 1, 2 or 3 to switch between the paired devices.
