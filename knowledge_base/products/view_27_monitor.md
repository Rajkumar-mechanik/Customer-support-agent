# Brightwell View 27 Monitor (BW-MN27)

## Overview
A 27 inch QHD IPS monitor with a 75 Hz refresh rate, 99 percent sRGB colour and a height-adjustable stand. The Brightwell price is ₹17,499. Warranty: 36 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Panel: 27 inch IPS, 2560 by 1440 pixels (QHD), 75 Hz, 5 ms response
- Colour: 99 percent sRGB, 350 nits brightness
- Ports: 1 HDMI 2.0, 1 DisplayPort 1.4, 1 USB-C with 65 W power delivery, 3.5 mm audio
- Stand: height, tilt and swivel adjustable, VESA 100 by 100 compatible
- Eye care: flicker-free backlight and low blue light mode
- Power: 30 W typical

## What is in the box
- Monitor and stand
- HDMI cable
- USB-C cable
- Power cable
- Quick start guide

## Setup
1. Attach the stand and connect power and a video cable.
2. Select the input from the menu button on the back, or use Auto in the menu.
3. Set the resolution to 2560 by 1440 and the refresh rate to 75 Hz in your computer's display settings.

## Troubleshooting
### No signal on the screen
Check that the correct input is selected, reseat the cable at both ends and try another cable or port. Connect the computer first and then power on the monitor.

### Screen flickers
Use the DisplayPort or HDMI cable from the box, set the refresh rate to 75 Hz and update the graphics driver. Turn off adaptive sync in the monitor menu to test.

### Colours look washed out
Select the sRGB picture mode, reset the monitor to factory settings and check that the HDMI range is set to Full in the graphics control panel.

### USB-C does not charge my laptop
The USB-C port supplies up to 65 W. A laptop that needs a larger charger will charge slowly while in use. Use the USB-C cable from the box, which supports video and power.

## Frequently asked questions
### How many dead pixels are acceptable?
A monitor with 3 or more dead pixels is eligible for replacement within 10 days of delivery.

### Can the monitor be wall-mounted?
Yes. It is VESA 100 by 100 compatible.

### Does the monitor have speakers?
No. Use the 3.5 mm audio output for headphones or speakers.
