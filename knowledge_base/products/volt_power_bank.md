# Brightwell Volt 20000mAh Power Bank (BW-PB20)

## Overview
A 20000 mAh power bank with 22.5 W fast charging, two USB-A ports and one USB-C port. The Brightwell price is ₹2,299. Warranty: 18 months from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Capacity: 20000 mAh (74 Wh)
- Output: USB-C up to 22.5 W, USB-A up to 18 W, combined 22.5 W
- Input: USB-C 18 W, full recharge in about 6 hours
- Safety: over-charge, over-current, short-circuit and temperature protection
- Carry-on: 74 Wh is within the 100 Wh airline limit for hand baggage
- Weight: 395 grams

## What is in the box
- Power bank
- USB-C to USB-C cable (30 cm)
- Pouch
- User manual

## Setup
1. Charge the power bank fully before first use using a 18 W or higher USB-C adapter.
2. Press the button once to see the battery level; four lights equal full charge.
3. Connect your device and the power bank starts charging automatically.

## Troubleshooting
### Power bank will not charge
Try a different USB-C cable and a 18 W adapter. The indicator lights should start blinking within one minute. If nothing happens, leave it connected for 30 minutes.

### Phone charges slowly
Use the USB-C port and the cable in the box. The USB-A ports share power and fast-charge only when used alone. Cheap cables can limit speed.

### Power bank gets warm
Mild warmth during fast charging is normal. If it becomes too hot to hold, swells, smells or smokes, stop using it and contact Brightwell Support immediately.

### Device stops charging after a few minutes
Very low-draw devices such as earbuds can make the power bank switch off. Press the button twice quickly to turn on low-current mode.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can I take it on a flight?
Yes in hand baggage only. At 74 Wh it is under the common 100 Wh limit. Never pack it in checked baggage.

### How many times can it charge my phone?
About 3 to 4 full charges for a typical 5000 mAh phone battery, depending on the phone and cable.

### Can I charge it and my phone at the same time?
Yes, this is called pass-through charging, but it is slower and runs warmer.
