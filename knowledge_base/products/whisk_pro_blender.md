# Brightwell Whisk Pro Blender 900W (BW-BL900)

## Overview
A 900 W countertop blender with a 1.5 litre BPA-free jar, six speeds and a pulse function for smoothies, chutneys and crushing ice. The Brightwell price is ₹4,299. Warranty: 24 months on the motor and 6 months on jars and blades from the delivery date.

## Specifications
- Motor: 900 W, up to 22000 rpm
- Jar: 1.5 litre BPA-free Tritan with stainless steel six-blade assembly
- Controls: 6 speeds, pulse and a 60 second auto-stop timer
- Safety: lid-lock and thermal overload protection
- Noise: about 82 dB at full speed
- Weight: 3.2 kg

## What is in the box

## Setup
1. Wash the jar, lid and blade assembly in warm soapy water before first use and dry them.
2. Place the jar on the base and turn it clockwise until it locks.
3. Start on speed 1 and increase gradually. Do not run for more than 60 seconds at a time.

## Troubleshooting
### Blender does not start
Check that the jar is twisted fully clockwise until it clicks and the lid is closed, because the lid-lock prevents starting otherwise. Make sure the plug is firmly in the socket and the thermal protection has not tripped.

### Blender stops during use and smells hot
The thermal overload protection has switched the motor off. Unplug it, let it cool for 30 minutes, reduce the quantity and add more liquid before restarting.

### Jar leaks from the bottom
Check the rubber gasket under the blade assembly is seated correctly and not cracked. Tighten the assembly and avoid filling above the 1.2 litre mark when blending hot liquids.

### Blades are not cutting well
Do not overfill the jar. Add liquid first, then solids, and use the tamper while blending thick mixtures. Dull blades can be replaced as an accessory.

## Frequently asked questions
### Can it crush ice?
Yes. Use the pulse function in short bursts with at least some liquid in the jar.

### Is the jar dishwasher safe?
The jar and lid are top-rack dishwasher safe. Wash the motor base only with a damp cloth.

### Can I blend hot soup?
Yes up to the 1.2 litre mark, and always vent the lid slightly by removing the centre cap.
