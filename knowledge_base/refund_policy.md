# Returns and Refund Policy

## Return window
You can return any product within 10 days of the delivery date. The 10-day window is counted from the day the courier marks the parcel delivered. Requests raised after day 10 cannot be accepted as a change of mind, but a manufacturing defect is still covered under our warranty policy.

## Conditions for a return
The product must be unused, in its original packaging, with all accessories, manuals and the invoice. Footwear must be unworn, with the tags attached and the shoe box intact. Earbuds, headphones and other in-ear products can be returned only if the hygiene seal is unbroken, unless the product is defective or was delivered damaged. Products with signs of use, physical damage caused after delivery, or missing parts are not eligible for a change-of-mind return.

## How to request a return
1. Open My Orders and select the item you want to return.
2. Choose Request Return and pick a reason.
3. For a damaged or defective item, upload clear photos or a short video showing the problem.
4. Choose a pickup slot. Pickup is free and is scheduled within 2 business days. The courier makes up to 3 pickup attempts.
5. Hand over the item in its original packaging along with the invoice.

## When the refund is issued
After the returned item reaches our warehouse, inspection takes up to 2 business days. Once approved, the refund is sent to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. Credit and debit cards, UPI and net banking refunds take 5 to 7 business days to appear, depending on your bank. Refunds to a Brightwell Wallet are credited within 24 hours of approval.

## Refunds for cash on delivery orders
COD refunds are paid by bank transfer within 7 to 10 business days after approval. You will receive a secure form to enter your bank account details. Brightwell Support never asks for bank details, card numbers or OTPs over chat, phone or email.

## What is refunded
The full item price is refunded. The shipping fee is refunded only when the return is caused by our error, such as a wrong, defective or damaged item. For a change-of-mind return the shipping fee is not refunded. There is no restocking fee.

## Returns that are rejected
If the returned item shows use, damage after delivery or missing parts, the return is rejected and the item is sent back to you at no charge. We email the inspection photos and the reason for rejection within 2 business days. You can reply to that email within 3 days to contest the decision.

## Checking refund status
Open My Orders, select the item and choose Return Status to see each stage: pickup scheduled, picked up, received, approved and refund issued. If the refund has not appeared after 7 business days from approval, contact Brightwell Support with your order ID and the refund reference. Your bank can also trace it using the acquirer reference number shown on the refund confirmation.

## Size and variant exchanges
Footwear and backpacks can be exchanged for another size or colour within 10 days of delivery, subject to stock. The exchange follows the same return conditions. If the new size is out of stock we refund the item instead.
