# Replacement and Warranty Policy

## Dead on arrival and defective items
If a product is broken, defective or does not work out of the box, report it within 10 days of delivery. You can choose a replacement or a full refund. We ask for photos or a short video showing the fault and, where relevant, the serial number. After we verify the report, the replacement is shipped within 3 business days. Pickup of the faulty unit is free.

## Replacement out of stock
If the same product is out of stock we offer an equivalent model of equal or higher specification when available, or a full refund. We never charge a difference in price for a replacement caused by a defect.

## Standard warranty period
Brightwell electronics and home appliances carry a 12-month warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date. Footwear and backpacks carry a 6-month warranty against manufacturing defects. Batteries, chargers, cables and other accessories carry a 6-month warranty. A product page may state a longer warranty, in which case the product page applies.

## How to make a warranty claim
Contact Brightwell Support with your order ID, the serial number printed on the box or the device label, and photos or a video of the problem. We diagnose the case within 2 business days. If a repair is needed, it takes 7 to 10 business days including transit. If the product cannot be repaired, we replace it with the same or an equivalent model.

## What the warranty does not cover
Physical damage such as drops and cracks, liquid or moisture damage beyond the stated water-resistance rating, damage from unauthorised repair or opening of the product, damage from non-Brightwell or uncertified chargers, normal wear such as worn shoe soles and fading, and cosmetic scratches that do not affect function.

## Warranty after repair or replacement
A repair does not extend the original warranty period. A replacement unit carries the remaining warranty of the original product or 3 months, whichever is longer.

## Pickup and service options
Free pickup for repairs is available in metro cities. In other locations we send a prepaid courier label. Please back up your data and remove personal accounts from tablets and smart devices before sending them, because repairs can require a factory reset.

## Proof of purchase
Your order confirmation email and the invoice in My Orders are valid proof of purchase. You do not need to keep the original box for a warranty claim, but you do need it for a change-of-mind return.
