# Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Order processing
Orders placed before 2 PM IST on a business day are packed and handed to the courier the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM, or on Sundays and public holidays, are dispatched on the next business day. Every order is dispatched within 24 hours of confirmation unless the item is shown as backordered on the product page.

## Delivery timelines
Standard delivery takes 2 to 4 business days to metro cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune), 4 to 7 business days to other cities and towns, and 7 to 10 business days to remote locations. Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days and is available only in metro cities. The estimated delivery date is shown at checkout and in the order confirmation email.

## Shipping charges
Standard delivery is free on orders of ₹999 and above. Orders below ₹999 pay a standard delivery fee of ₹59. Express delivery costs ₹149 on every order. Brightwell does not ship outside India.

## Tracking your order
When your order is dispatched we send an email and SMS with a tracking link. You can also open My Orders, select the order and choose Track. Courier status updates every 12 hours. If tracking shows no movement for more than 24 hours after dispatch, this is usually a scanning delay at a courier hub and the parcel is still moving.

## Late or delayed deliveries
Courier delays happen, so please allow up to 3 business days after the estimated delivery date before raising a delay complaint. If your order is more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date, contact Brightwell Support with your order ID. We open an investigation with the courier and reply within 24 hours. If the courier cannot locate the parcel we will either ship a replacement at no cost or refund you in full, whichever you prefer.

## Lost parcels
A parcel is declared lost when it is still undelivered 10 business days after the estimated delivery date, or earlier if the courier confirms the loss. For a lost parcel you can choose a free reshipment or a full refund. Refunds for lost parcels are issued within 5 to 7 business days to the original payment method.

## Marked delivered but not received
If tracking says delivered but you have not received the parcel, report it within 48 hours of the delivery scan. Check with neighbours and building security first. We will contact the courier and share the outcome of the investigation within 3 business days. If the delivery cannot be proved, we reship or refund.

## Failed delivery attempts
The courier makes up to 3 delivery attempts. After the third failed attempt the parcel is held at the courier hub for 7 days and then returned to our warehouse. Once the parcel is back, we refund the order amount within 5 to 7 business days or reship it if you ask us to.

## Damaged in transit
If the outer box is visibly damaged when the courier arrives, you can refuse the delivery. If you notice the damage after opening, report it within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the outer box, the packing material and the product. We then ship a replacement within 3 business days or issue a full refund, at your choice. Return pickup is free for transit damage.

## Changing the delivery address
The delivery address can be edited from My Orders only until the order is dispatched. After dispatch the address cannot be changed, but the courier may allow one redirection to another address in the same city when you contact them using the tracking link.
